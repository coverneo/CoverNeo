Service Information

CoverNeo provides independent device setup, data-transfer and general technology assistance. Services are subject to device compatibility, software version and technical conditions.

Customers should maintain a backup of important personal data before starting any setup or transfer service.

Customers must personally enter passwords, PINs, OTPs and other confidential information. CoverNeo does not request or retain such credentials.

Data-transfer results may vary depending on the devices, operating systems, applications and available storage.

These services are limited to setup, guidance and basic assistance and do not include hardware repair, professional data recovery or cybersecurity services.
