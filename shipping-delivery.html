Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

At CoverNeo, we aim to deliver your products safely and on time across India.

Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after successful order confirmation and payment verification, where applicable.

Processing time may vary depending on product availability and order volume.

Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time will depend on the customer's location, courier service, product availability, and other operational factors.

Any delivery timeline displayed at checkout or communicated at the time of order is an estimate and may be affected by circumstances beyond our control.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be communicated or displayed before order confirmation.

Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact details.

CoverNeo will not be responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer.

Delayed or Lost Orders

If an order is delayed, damaged, or appears to be lost during transit, customers should contact CoverNeo as soon as possible so that the matter can be checked with the delivery partner.

Contact Us

For shipping-related questions, please contact CoverNeo through the contact information provided on our website.
