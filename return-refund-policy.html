Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

At CoverNeo, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase.

Return Eligibility

A product may be eligible for return or replacement if it is:

  • Received damaged.
  • Defective or not functioning as described.
  • Incorrectly delivered.
  • Significantly different from the product ordered.

Products must be returned in their original condition and packaging where applicable.

Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene, customization, usage, or other applicable restrictions. The return eligibility of each product may be communicated at the time of purchase.

Return Request

Customers should contact CoverNeo within the applicable return period communicated with the order, providing the order details and, where necessary, photographs or videos showing the issue.

Inspection

Returned products may be inspected before a replacement or refund is approved.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the original payment method or another appropriate method, subject to applicable payment-provider timelines.

Replacement

Where applicable, CoverNeo may offer a replacement instead of a refund, subject to product availability.

Shipping for Returns

Return shipping arrangements may depend on the reason for the return and the applicable return conditions.

Contact Us

For return or refund requests, please contact CoverNeo using the contact details provided on our website.

Note: Return, replacement, and refund eligibility may vary by product and order. The applicable terms communicated at the time of purchase will apply.
